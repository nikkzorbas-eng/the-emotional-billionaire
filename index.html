The Method

Intelligence Under Pressure

"Intelligence Under Pressure is the ability to recognize what is happening, maintain composure, think clearly, and execute effectively when circumstances challenge your ability to do so."

READ→COMPOSE→EXECUTE

The operating loop runs in seconds: read the moment as it actually is, compose yourself back to center, then execute — cleanly, at full stakes.

Ability is what you have. Access is what pressure leaves you. The method protects the access.
